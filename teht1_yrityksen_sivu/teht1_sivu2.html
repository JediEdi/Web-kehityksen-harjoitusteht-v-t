Size: larger cubes are more suitable as objects of worship. Smaller ones are portable.

Weight: heavier cubes are secure from burglar and heretic actions alike. Lighter ones have been proven to negate allergies.

Chemical makeup: To keep the prices consumer-friendly, we elect to use an alloy of tungsten-adjacent metals. The ratio between them is up to the customer.
